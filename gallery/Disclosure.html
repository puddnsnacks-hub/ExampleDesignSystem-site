1.1How each application review status is decided
  • Received — the application is logged and assigned a tracking number within one business day.
  • In review — a program officer checks eligibility, completeness, and budget against the notice.
  • More information needed — the applicant has 30 days to supply what is missing before the file closes.
  • Recommended — the review panel has scored the application and forwarded it for final selection.
  • Awarded — funds are obligated and the grant agreement is issued to the recipient.

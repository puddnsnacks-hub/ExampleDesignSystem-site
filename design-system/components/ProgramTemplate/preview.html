Grants and funding

Bridge Investment Program

Competitive grants to replace, rehabilitate and preserve bridges in poor or fair condition.

On this page

  • Eligibility

Overview

The Bridge Investment Program awards up to $2.4 billion this year to replace, rehabilitate and preserve bridges in poor condition, with a set-aside for bridges owned by counties, tribes and small towns.

Since 2022 the program has funded work on 617 bridges in 48 states. Awards are competitive; the evaluation weighs the condition of the bridge, the traffic it carries, the cost of the detour when it is closed, and how ready the applicant is to start construction.

Eligibility

States, metropolitan planning organizations, local governments, tribal governments and federal land management agencies may apply. Read the full eligibility list before you start. Applicants must own or operate the bridge, or hold a written agreement with the owner.

Who can apply

  • State departments of transportation
  • Metropolitan planning organizations
  • Units of local government, including counties and special districts
  • Federally recognized tribal governments
  • Federal land management agencies, on their own or with a state

What is funded

  • Replacement of a bridge rated in poor condition
  • Rehabilitation that returns a bridge to fair or good condition
    • Deck replacement and superstructure repair
    • Scour countermeasures and seismic retrofit
  • Protection of a bridge in fair condition from falling to poor

A bridge that is closed is not a transportation asset. It is a detour, and the detour is paid for every day by the people who live on the wrong side of it.

Office of Highways, condition report, 2025

How condition is rated

Inspectors rate the deck, superstructure and substructure on a 0 to 9 scale; the lowest of the three sets the bridge's condition. A rating of 4 or below is poor (23 CFR 490.409).

How to apply

  1. Register in SAM.gov and Grants.gov. Registration can take four weeks.
  2. Download the notice and the application template below.
  3. Submit through Grants.gov using the opportunity number EXA-HWY-BIP-2026 before 11:59 p.m. Eastern on October 1, 2026.

Questions about the program

Who is eligible to apply?
State departments of transportation, metropolitan planning organizations, tribal governments, and units of local government that own or operate the bridge.
When does the application window close?
Applications are due 11:59 p.m. Eastern on October 1, 2026. Late submissions are not reviewed.
Can I edit an application after submitting?
No. Withdraw and resubmit before the deadline.
Is there a match requirement?
The federal share is 50 percent for large projects and 80 percent for projects under $100 million. Off-system bridges owned by a county or tribe may be funded at up to 100 percent.

Why we ask for this, and how it is used
  • Mailing address — where the printed permit is sent. It does not have to match your billing address, and it is not shared with any other agency.
  • Delivery method — first-class mail is included in the permit fee; express courier is charged at the payment step and needs a street address, not a PO box.
  • Email — used only to send the confirmation number and delivery updates for this application. You will not be added to a mailing list.
  • Saving and finishing later keeps this application for 30 days; after that the entries are deleted.
  • Required fields are marked with an asterisk. Everything else is optional and can be left blank.
